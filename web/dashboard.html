Non-stock items to consider stocking

Non-stock item-locations (stockable N) at the branches with usage in the last 12 completed months. Usage is what shipped from the branch's stock, by month, oldest first in the trend. Steadiness is how much monthly usage swings: steady under 0.5 (coefficient of variation), variable under 1, lumpy above. Customers, orders and sales are customer order lines over the same 12 months; direct ship / special is the share of those lines that never touched stock. Export for P21 writes one file per branch, stockable_<location>.txt, each line the item ID, a tab, and Y, with no header, for import. Making an item stockable doesn't set its min and max: set those too, or it won't reorder.

Turns, GMROI and days on hand by

Sales, COGS and gross profit are the last 12 months of invoices for item-locations you stock today (direct ships and items with no stock on hand are left out, so this matches the Days on hand screen). Turns = COGS ÷ cost on hand. GMROI = gross profit ÷ cost on hand. Both use today's cost on hand in place of a 12-month average, so they'll firm up as snapshots build. Days on hand is workdays of supply at current usage, weighted by cost, so it rolls up across items with different units.

Stockouts by usage class

What's behind the numbers

    By month

    Stockable items (not class NS) at the branches. Transactions = WO transactions with a quantity. Restocked: counted in the month the item was last at zero, once it's restocked. Incl. still out adds stockouts not restocked yet, in the month they started, leaving out items whose max was 0 when they ran out (not meant to be restocked). Class = the item's class on the date of each transaction, from the inv_loc audit trail; usage class is the first letter (AA–AD count as A), Other = XX, N##, S### and blank. Days out run from the transaction that took it to zero to the next one above zero (still out: to today). Order lines are customer order lines for stocked items (not direct ship or special order). Today's figures use max above 0. Location filters apply; supplier, buyer and ABC filters don't.

    Stockouts by

    Each stockout is when on hand hit zero at a branch in the last 180 days, for stocked items. Lost sales = days out × average daily usage × the item's 12-month average selling price, so treat it as an estimate. Lead-time demand uses the supplier's median lead time from the scorecard.

    Fill rate by week

    Customer order lines for stocked items by the week they were entered. Filled = shipped complete within 2 business days. Location filters apply · double-click a week for its locations.

    Fill rate by

    Customer fill: order lines entered in the last 180 days for stocked items (stockable, with a max, not class NS) at the line's ship-from location. A line is filled when its full quantity shipped within 2 business days of being entered (pick ticket ship quantities). Direct-ship and special-order lines, quotes, RMAs, lines the customer requested for a later date, and lines canceled with nothing shipped are left out. Holidays count as business days. Supplier delivery: PO lines for stocked items entered in the last 180 days, once received in full or past due. On time in full = the full quantity was received by the line's due date.

    Vendor reorder focus

    A late-reorder stockout is one where nothing was on order when the item ran out, it ran out while a PO was open but not yet due, or the min didn't cover lead-time demand. A stockout before the PO was due doesn't always mean the PO was placed late: a demand spike, a long due date or a transfer can cause it too. Stockouts where an open PO was past due are the supplier's lateness and are shown separately. Ranking = estimated lost sales from late reordering + items below min with nothing on order today (each counted at the average lost sales per event). “Below min right now” lists every item-location whose on hand − allocated + in transit + on PO is at or below its P21 min.
